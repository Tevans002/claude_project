Gale-Shapley(preference lists for n hospitals and n students)
    Let M be an empty matching
    While some hospital h is unmatched and hasn’t proposed to every student:
        Let s be the first student on h’s list to whom h has not yet proposed
        If s is unmatched:
            Add (h, s) to M
        Else s is matched to h′:
            If s prefers h to h′:
                Remove (h′, s) from M
                Add (h, s) to M
            Else s prefers h′ to h:
                Do nothing
    Return M

Stable Matching tool design (pattern to reuse):
- Preference tables on the left, pseudocode on the right with a red box on the
  current line, M box showing the matching, and a log line for feedback.
- The user advances by clicking the correct item in the table. Yes/No buttons
  handle decisions. Wrong clicks show a short explanation of why.
- Automatic steps animate with ~1s pauses. Clicks are blocked meanwhile.
- A multiple-choice quiz unlocks at the end with distractors that teach a
  specific mistake. Reset and "New problem" (cycles presets) buttons.
- Click handlers are attached to the tables (event delegation) so they survive
  re-rendering.
